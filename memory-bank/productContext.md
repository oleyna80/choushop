# Product Context

## Why This Product Exists

ChouShop sells curated surprise boxes for customers who want a cute, giftable, low-friction purchase without choosing every item individually.

The product is designed for fast conversion: a customer should understand the mood of a box, trust the checkout, and buy in a short mobile session.

## Customer Problem

Customers want gifts or personal treats that feel curated and delightful, but choosing individual small items takes time and removes the surprise.

The store solves this by offering clear box themes with enough information to build trust while preserving surprise.

## Product Feel

The storefront should feel:

- premium-cute;
- clean;
- glossy but not childish;
- soft luxury;
- mobile-first;
- fast to purchase;
- trustworthy.

The interface should avoid heavy text, clutter, and generic marketplace patterns.

## Conversion Principles

- Show clear box value quickly.
- Make CTA visible and direct.
- Explain delivery and returns before payment.
- Show prices in EUR TTC.
- Avoid unnecessary checkout steps.
- Use French copy for customer-facing pages.
- Do not overwhelm customers with configuration.

## Unique Value

- Mystery with a clear theme.
- Giftable products.
- France-first checkout and legal posture.
- Small curated catalog.
- Operational foundation ready for future agents.

## Product Boundaries

MVP-0 is not a marketplace, customer portal, AI shopping assistant, or advanced OMS.

The product must first become a reliable selling machine. Automation comes later.
